### Mar - April 2026

OpenFF will be hosting a series of workshops in March-April 2026. These are interactive 1 hour Zoom sessions led by the creators and maintainers of OpenFF tools, focused on specific topics of interest.

[Find more details here](https://docs.openforcefield.org/en/latest/workshops/2026/index.html) or [click here to register!](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScPL35xwbA4wSJ0dNbFE5rm4-ojQC2MQIsvROJJXClbJIaDVw/viewform?usp=publish-editor)
